# Sales Analysis Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard analyzing sales and profit by product category, country, customer, and sales manager.

## Key Metrics (2020 view)
- Total Sales: 56.74M
- Total Profit: 9.51M
- Profit Margin: about 17%
- Total Orders: 510

## Features
- KPI cards for total sales, total profit, profit margin, and total orders
- Sales vs profit by category, profit by category (waterfall), and sales vs country
- Sales by customer (treemap) and sales by sales manager
- Monthly sales trend and a cost-to-sales ratio vs profit scatter plot
- Country by category matrix
- Slicers for year, country, order category, and category
